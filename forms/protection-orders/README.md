# Protection order forms

These are the official Indiana forms for asking a court for a protection order, and for the steps after. They come from the Indiana courts' [Protection, No Contact and Workplace Violence Restraining Orders](https://www.in.gov/courts/iocs/publications/po-forms/) page and are stored here unchanged. The tool doesn't fill these yet.

- These are the Indiana courts' own forms (they print their numbers as OJA-PO-0100 and so on). They are not Coalition for Court Access forms.
- None of these PDFs has fill-in fields. They are printed and filled in by hand. The official page also has most of them as Word files, which can be typed in.
- The instructions (PO-0102 and PO-0103) say to take the completed forms to the Clerk's office. The courts also take petitions online through the Protection Order E-filing Service, and trained advocates can prepare and file the petition with the person. See [Getting a Protection Order](https://www.in.gov/courts/selfservice/protection-orders/) and [E-filing Protection Orders](https://www.in.gov/courts/help/efiling/protection-orders/). The courts' site encourages getting help from an advocate, because asking for an order can make some situations more dangerous.
- Each form prints its approval and revision dates. The official page asks people to check those dates against its list and download again if a form has been updated.
- Not included: the workplace violence restraining order forms (WV-0100 to WV-0107), which an employer files, and the Protection Order Deskbook and law texts. They are on the official page.
