# Paternity forms

These are Indiana Legal Help's forms for asking a court to establish paternity, approved by the Coalition for Court Access and stored here unchanged. The tool doesn't fill these yet.

| File | Forms inside (form number) | Pages | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [petition-for-paternity.pdf](petition-for-paternity.pdf) | Instructions; Appearance by Self-Represented Person in Civil Case (CCA-DC-0519-3018); confidential information form (CCA-DC-0320-1069); Verified Petition to Establish Paternity (CCA-JP-0519-1063); Summons (CCA-JP-0519-1064); Order Setting Hearing (CCA-JP-0519-1065); Order Establishing Paternity, Custody, Child Support, and Parenting Time (CCA-JP-0519-1066) | 21 (instructions on 1–6) | [Petition for Paternity](https://indianalegalhelp.org/legal-topic/petition-for-paternity/) ([original PDF](https://indianalegalhelp.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Paternity-Petition-202510.pdf)) |
| [waiver-of-service.pdf](waiver-of-service.pdf) | Paternity Waiver of Service (CCA-JP-0224-1067) | 2 | [Paternity Waiver of Service](https://indianalegalhelp.org/legal-topic/paternity-waiver-of-service/) ([original PDF](https://indianalegalhelp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Paternity-Waiver-of-Service-202402.pdf)) |

Both have fill-in fields. Indiana Legal Help also has forms for starting or changing parenting time, custody and child support. They aren't included here.

Downloaded November 3, 2026. Indiana Legal Help may post newer versions, so check the official source before filing.
