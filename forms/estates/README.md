# Estate forms

This is Indiana Legal Help's Small Estate Affidavit, approved by the Coalition for Court Access and stored here unchanged. The tool doesn't fill it yet.

| File | Form (form number) | Pages | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [small-estate-affidavit.pdf](small-estate-affidavit.pdf) | Affidavit for Transfer of Assets Without Administration (CCA-EM-0722-5000) | 2 | [Small Estate](https://indianalegalhelp.org/legal-topic/small-estate/) ([original PDF](https://indianalegalhelp.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/5000_SmallEstateAffidavit_0722_FILLABLE-1.pdf)) |

It has fill-in fields. It is for estates worth $100,000 or less. Indiana Legal Help says it is not filed with the court. Instead, it goes to whoever holds the property, like a bank.

This is the only official self-help estate form we found. We found no forms for estate cases filed in court, like opening an estate or probating a will, on Indiana Legal Help or on the Indiana courts' forms pages. Indiana Legal Help's [Probate Frequently Asked Questions](https://indianalegalhelp.org/probate-frequently-asked-questions/) strongly recommends doing probate with an attorney.

Downloaded November 3, 2026. Indiana Legal Help may post newer versions, so check the official source before filing.
